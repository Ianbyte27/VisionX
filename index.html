Talent is everywhere. Access to markets is not.

Creative people and businesses often can’t find the customers, partners, equipment or opportunities they need. VISIONX puts them in one place.

  • Musicians struggle to find producers and songwriters.
  • Artists search for affordable beats and instrumentals.
  • Creators struggle to find companies that need advertising.
  • Event organisers can’t find stages, sound and lighting.
  • Fashion designers can’t reach international customers.
  • Filmmakers look for actors, editors and production partners.
  • Businesses can’t identify the right creative professionals.
  • Talented people in developing countries lack access to global markets.

Eight divisions. One connected platform.

Instead of separate apps for each industry, VISIONX brings them together so a project can move from a song to a video to a shoot to a launch without leaving the platform.

VISIONX Music & Entertainment

A global marketplace for lyrics, beats, songwriting, production, mixing, mastering, DJ and artist bookings, studio time and licensing.

A musician in Malawi buys a beat from a producer in the UK, then collaborates with a songwriter in South Africa.

VISIONX Film & Video

Directors, actors, editors, cinematographers and producers, plus music videos, documentaries, adverts and film equipment rentals.

VISIONX Fashion

Designers, brands, models, stylists and photographers. Clothing can be sold internationally or rented for photoshoots, music videos and campaigns.

VISIONX Creators

Influencers, YouTubers, TikTok and Instagram creators, photographers and designers. Businesses compare portfolios, audiences and prices, then book.

A clothing company in France finds a creator in Malawi or South Africa to advertise its products.

VISIONX Events & Equipment

Stages, sound systems, lighting, LED screens, generators, tents, decoration and technical teams, with offers to compare and bookings to confirm.

Equipment owners find customers. Organisers stop needing to own everything.

VISIONX Marketplace

One place to advertise, buy, sell, rent and book physical and digital products, services and equipment, with search, price comparison and portfolios.

VISIONX Connect

The collaboration network: private messaging, invitations, file sharing, group conversations and project tracking.

An artist in Zimbabwe finds an artist in South Africa, sends an invite, and they build a song together.

VISIONX Opportunities

Freelance jobs, auditions and casting, sponsorships, brand partnerships, competitions, employment and international business openings.

How VISIONX will grow

  1. Foundation

    Finalise the business model, validate demand, settle ownership and intellectual property, build the team and design the platform.

  2. Initial platform

    Registration, profiles, service listings, music buying and selling, search, messaging and bookings.

  3. Expansion

    Fashion marketplace, creator bookings, film and video services, events and equipment rentals, the Collaboration Hub and more payment options.

  4. International growth

    New countries, more languages and currencies, global brand relationships, investors and commercial partners.

Origin: Malawi. Planned hubs for partnerships and growth: United Kingdom, United States and France.
